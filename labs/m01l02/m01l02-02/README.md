## Goal

You can explain what the business source licence changed in twenty twenty three, what OpenTofu is and where it came from, who owns Terraform now, and choose between the two tools for a given team with reasons.

In the lesson: In August of twenty twenty three, HashiCorp changed the licence of Terraform from the Mozilla Public Licence, which is an open source licence, to the Business Source Licence, which is not. Read the two lines on screen carefully, because the difference is narrower than the reaction suggested. Using Terraform inside your own company, on your own infrastructure, for your own products, is still free and still permitted. What the new licence forbids is taking Terraform and selling a service that competes with HashiCorp's own. There is also a clock: each release converts to the open source licence four years after it is published. For the overwhelming majority of working engineers, nothing about their daily use became illegal. What changed was the guarantee.
